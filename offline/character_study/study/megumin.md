# Megumin

**Basis:** This first-pass study is drawn from Volume 1 (v1 L398–2627), where Megumin is introduced, recruited, and established as a core party member; Volume 2 Section 2 (v2 L2628–3078), the Snow Sprite hunt, Kazuma's death and resurrection, and the tavern confrontation that sets up the party swap; Volume 2 Section 3 (v2 L3229–3507), which covers the party-swap aftermath, the dungeon-trip proposal, and the party's escort to Keele's Dungeon; and Volume 2 Section 4 (v2 L3579–3760), the aftermath of the Keele dungeon dive and the visit to Wiz's shop. All claims are tied to the cited text; observations of Megumin are filtered through Kazuma's first-person narration. Section 3 includes substantial non-Megumin material (Kazuma's temporary party escaping the Rookie Killer, and Kazuma and Aqua's dungeon dive), so Megumin's relevant appearances there are the reunion scene (v2 L3309–3335), the dungeon discussion (v2 L3322–3335), the dungeon-entrance send-off (v2 L3379–3385), and one aside about her skepticism toward Aqua (v2 L3427). Section 4 is likewise largely non-Megumin material (Kazuma and Aqua's dungeon dive and the Wiz shop scene), so Megumin's relevant appearances there are the cabin reunion (v2 L3658–3661), the note of her unexplained early-morning absence (v2 L3673–3676), and Kazuma's party-balance assessment of her combat role (v2 L3677–3681).

## Identity and background

- Her opening self-introduction: "My name is Megumin! My calling is that of an Archwizard, and one who controls the strongest offensive magic, Explosion…!" (v1 L478). She also describes herself as "an elite mage from the Crimson Demon Clan" (v1 L485).
- Race: Crimson Demon. Aqua explains that Crimson Demons are "born with high intellect and strong magic power, with most of them having the potential to become powerful mages. They're well known for their red eyes and their strange names" (v1 L492).
- Family: her mother is Yuiyui and her father is Hyoizaburo (v1 L498). She is defensive about her own name and her parents' names (v1 L496, L502, L1510-1521).
- Job: Archwizard, a top-tier job. Her adventurer's card is genuine, showing "a top-tier job with the ability to use powerful spells," and "the magic stat on the card's very high" (v1 L504).
- Level: she says at recruitment, "My level's just six" (v1 L593).
- Circumstances when she appears: she is penniless and starving — "I haven't eaten in three days; may you give me something to eat before the interview…?" (v1 L485) — and cannot find a party: "Please don't ditch me! No team's willing to take me!" (v1 L595).
- By Volume 2, Kazuma summarizes her in shorthand as "a chuuni Archwizard named Megumin" (v2 L2679) and "the Crimson Demon mage with an eye-patch" (v2 L2681), showing that her Crimson Demon identity, chuunibyou presentation, and eyepatch are now stable, recognized traits within the party.
- She remains the party's tactical trump card in combat: after she has spent her spell, Kazuma notes "our trump card, Megumin, couldn't use magic any more today" (v2 L2806).
- In the party-swap aftermath, she is reintroduced in defeat: "Megumin was carried on Dust's back" (v2 L3309), having spent her only Explosion on a demonstration and been rendered useless for the fight that followed (v2 L3312).

## Physical traits

- Small and young-looking: "The small, frail girl who looked to be about 12-13" (v1 L477); "Her features were delicate like a porcelain doll–it was a loli" (v1 L475).
- Coloring and clothing: "red, sleepy eyes" (v1 L472), "smooth black hair reached her shoulders" (v1 L473), and "classic witch attire with a black cloak, dark boots, pointy hat and a staff" (v1 L474).
- Eyepatch: covers one eye (v1 L477). She first claims it restrains her power, then admits, "I made that up. This is just a normal eyepatch. It just looks nice" (v1 L491). She still wears it in Volume 2 (v2 L2681).
- Small/flat build: she presses "her lacking chest onto my back" (v1 L561). Kazuma later notices no sensation there and thinks, "Well, it was expected of a loli" (v1 L2120-2121).
- In action, she is physically taxed by non-magical combat: chasing Snow Sprites alongside Darkness, she is "panting" and finally takes one down "with her staff" rather than magic (v2 L2752-2753).
- After casting Explosion, she "laid prone on the snowy ground after exhausting her mana, displaying her adventurer's card proudly," and delivers her kill count while "half buried in the snow" (v2 L2760-2762). The visual is deliberately unglamorous despite her pride. In Section 3 the same exhausted state repeats off the battlefield: after showing off for Dust, she is "carried on Dust's back" (v2 L3309), drained to helplessness once her one spell is gone.

## Core obsession: Explosion

- She calls Explosion "the strongest offensive spell" (v1 L509) and "the ultimate attack spell" (v1 L527). In practice it disintegrates a giant toad and leaves "a crater twenty meters in diameter" (v1 L537).
- Her build is single-mindedly Explosion-focused. Asked what skills she has: "Of course, I learned explosion-related skills… Explosion, Explosion Damage Increase, Fast Chanting, et cetera. All the skills needed to cast the perfect explosion spell. It's the same as before and will be so in the future" (v1 L1318-1319). Asked about mid-tier magic: "I don't plan to" (v1 L1321).
- The obsession is framed as love, not efficiency. When told other spells would make adventuring easier: "However, I couldn't do it; my only love is Explosion. Even though I can only cast it once per day, even if I'll collapse after using the spell, my one true love is still Explosion! The only reason I chose the path of the Archwizard was to cast explosion spells" (v1 L580).
- She accepts the trade-offs: Explosion has "a long casting time" (v1 L509), and after one cast she collapses because her mana exceeds her limit (v1 L546-547). She can "only use Explosion; I don't know any other magic" (v1 L562).
- She spends earnings on making an already-overpowered spell stronger rather than diversifying: she hugs her upgraded manatite staff and grinds her face against it; the upgrade increases Explosion's power "by a dozen or so percent" (v1 L1343-1348).
- She actively tries to convert Kazuma: "Adventurer is the only job other than Archwizard that can use Explosion. I can teach you if you want to learn. Or rather, there are no other skills that are worth learning except Explosion, right? ... Come, let's walk the path of Explosion together!" (v1 L714).
- In the Snow Sprite hunt, she treats Explosion as the party's solution but defers to Kazuma before firing: "They're too fast, Kazuma. Darkness and I can't get them… Can I use Explosion to blow up an area?" (v2 L2752). Once authorized, she chants and releases "her powerful spell that could only be used once a day" (v2 L2758), producing a crater and killing eight (v2 L2759-2761).
- Even collapsed in the snow, she reports the result with pride: "Eight! I took out 8. And I raised a level!" (v2 L2761); Kazuma's wry aside is "You would've looked much cooler if you hadn't said that while half buried in the snow" (v2 L2762).
- The Volume 2 sequence adds an important limit: she is not Explosion-happy against every enemy. When Kazuma half-seriously proposes Explosion on the Winter Shogun for its 200-million bounty, she explains the fairy's immaterial nature and says, "Also, I don't want to cast Explosion on such a scary opponent" (v2 L2947). Her obsession has a boundary when the target is both resistant and terrifying.
- In the immediate aftermath of the party swap, Dust reports that Megumin volunteered her Explosion credentials, then "said, 'Let me show you my power', and suddenly used all her mana on an empty grass plain to cast her explosion spell" (v2 L3312). The trigger was praise: "I praised her for her abilities" (v2 L3312). This shows the same urge to display Explosion for approval even when it has no tactical purpose and leaves her useless afterward — she then has to be carried back on Dust's back (v2 L3309).
- The dungeon proposal reveals the boundary of her Explosion identity in reverse: she hates dungeons because Explosion "might cause the dungeon to collapse" and thus cannot be used there, reducing her to "a normal person" (v2 L3328).
- Kazuma's Section 4 assessment confirms both the power and the built-in weakness of this specialization: "Megumin's instant damage output was higher than most other mages, and could often resolve the problem with one spell," but "the current issue was the need for reliable damage output" (v2 L3680-3681). She is the party's high-burst finisher, not a source of sustained offense.

## Daily need / ritual

- Megumin has "something she needed to do every day, which was casting her Explosion" (v1 L1433), and she needs Kazuma along because no one else will carry her back afterward (v1 L1435). She will not cast too close to town because "the guards will lecture me again" about the noise (v1 L1439).
- She chooses the abandoned castle as a target: "Let's pick that place! No one will complain even if I destroy a ruin like that" (v1 L1453), and casts there "daily without fail" regardless of time or weather (v1 L1460-1464).
- She and Kazuma develop a shared vocabulary for evaluating explosions, rating "their sound (not just the volume, but other factors like the pitch as well)" (v1 L1471).
- When the Dullahan objects, she invents a biological justification: "Those of the Crimson Demon clan need to cast explosion spells once every day, or we'll die" — which Kazuma immediately rejects: "Hey, I've never heard of this before! Stop bullshitting me!" (v1 L1526-1527).
- Sections 2 and 3 do not depict the daily ritual; instead, the one-cast-per-day constraint is restated in quest contexts (v2 L2758, L2806, L3312). The result is the same vulnerability: once Megumin fires, she is spent and must be protected or carried.
- Section 4 adds an ambiguous data point: Kazuma notes, "Megumin went somewhere early in the morning. She would disappear like that sometimes. I wonder where she goes?" (v2 L3675-3676). The section does not identify the errand, and it is not explicitly tied to her Explosion ritual here — which is itself noteworthy against v1 L1435, where she says she needs Kazuma to carry her back after casting. On this pass, it registers as an unexplained private habit and a small ongoing mystery from Kazuma's viewpoint.

## Behavior under pressure: public bravado, private responsibility

- Confronted by the angry Dullahan, Megumin is "intimidated and a bit scared," yet still flicks her cape and gives a grand speech reframing her daily explosions as a scheme: "I cast explosion magic continuously to lure you, the Demon King's General, out…! As I planned, you came to town alone after being tricked; your end is near!" (v1 L1508-1513). Kazuma and Aqua whisper that this is revisionist nonsense (v1 L1515-1516), and Megumin blushes while maintaining the pose (v1 L1518).
- She is quick to deflect blame when cornered: when everyone looks for the Explosion caster, she "turned her head sharply at a girl mage standing beside her," silently framing an innocent mage (v1 L1493-1495); when the Dullahan presses further, she hands the fight to Aqua by calling her "Master" (v1 L1534).
- But when Darkness is cursed while protecting her, Megumin's face turns pale (v1 L1557), and she immediately prepares to go alone: "I'm responsible for this incident. I'll head to the castle and cast Explosion on that Dullahan directly to dispel Darkness's curse" (v1 L1579). She then accepts Kazuma's help and softens: "you have to rely on me when the time comes" (v1 L1584).
- In Volume 2, the bravado collapses into survival instinct when the Winter Shogun appears: "Megumin who was just boasting of her victory was now playing dead" (v2 L2773-2774). Kazuma's dry admiration — "I felt a sense of awe from Aqua who prostrated without hesitation, and Megumin who was playing dead so perfectly" (v2 L2813) — frames her stillness as a comedic but effective dodge when her trump card is unavailable.
- The private-responsibility side emerges after Kazuma is beheaded. She is "lying on me sobbing" and calling, "… zuma…! Kazuma, get up! Kazuma!" (v2 L2903-2904). Aqua's comment, "That child is so stubborn, really" (v2 L2910), indicates Megumin refused to give up on Kazuma's body until he revived.
- When Kazuma awakens, "Megumin and Darkness noticed I had awakened, they wordlessly gave me a tight hug" (v2 L2914), and Megumin immediately asks, "… Is your body okay? Does it feel weird anywhere?" (v2 L2927). This is quiet, direct care, not theater.
- In the tavern when Dust insults Kazuma as the weakest-job dead weight, Megumin tries to keep the peace by placing her own feelings second: "Kazuma, don't bother with them. I won't mind no matter what they say about me." (v2 L2998-2999; the narration lists Megumin, Darkness, and Aqua in that order, making this first reassurance Megumin's). But when Kazuma retorts that there are "no pretty ladies," all three — Megumin included — "pointed at themselves and gasped" (v2 L3020), showing her pride is not fully suppressed.
- When Kazuma announces a dungeon trip the day after the swap, she protests bluntly — "Don't wanna." (v2 L3323) — and "tried to run" until Kazuma grabs her by the sleeve (v2 L3325). The avoidance is physical, not rhetorical, and immediately gives way to the deeper fear she then voices (v2 L3328).

## Conscience vs. title-chasing

- At the quarry, Megumin hesitates to blow up a boulder when she notices a defenseless wyvern hatchling nesting there (v1 L2600-2608). She "opted not to cast her spell, and dejectedly hung her head" (v1 L2608), and brightens when the engineer says she can wait until the wyvern leaves (v1 L2610-2612).
- But the moment Kazuma jokes that killing even a hatchling would let her call herself "Dragon Slayer," she immediately casts Explosion (v1 L2618-2620), then demands: "Kazuma, please call me 'Dragon Slayer Megumin' from now on" (v1 L2626). The scene pairs a genuine moral pause with an almost reflexive ambition for recognition.
- Volume 2 Section 2 does not repeat this moral dilemma, but it shows a related practical boundary: she will not throw Explosion at the Winter Shogun because the target frightens her and resists the spell, prioritizing survival and prudence over a 200-million trophy (v2 L2947).

## Goals, fears, and deep conflicts

- Stated goal: defeat the Demon King and become the strongest mage. "I'm Megumin! The top mage of the Crimson Demons, the master of explosion magic! For the Demon King to overlook me and call himself the strongest, I'll decimate him with my strongest spell!" (v1 L882). Later, when Kazuma proposes retiring from adventuring: "This is a problem for me too! I want to defeat the Demon King with Kazuma and gain the title of the strongest mage!" (v1 L2563).
- She frames herself as a future Demon King slayer and dragon slayer (v1 L2594, L2626).
- Fear: being unwanted or useless. She clings to Kazuma and bargains hard because "No team's willing to take me!" (v1 L595); she offers to work for meals rather than a reward share (v1 L589); she is momentarily stung by being called a loli (v1 L716-722); and she is startled by Kazuma's mumble about switching parties (v1 L1329-1331).
- Conflict: her romantic commitment to Explosion clashes with practical reality. Kazuma notes she cannot use Explosion in dungeons or indoors (v1 L594), and she is limited to one spell a day, yet she refuses to compromise (v1 L580, L1318-1321). The wyvern scene shows a second conflict between conscience and desired titles (v1 L2600-2626).
- Volume 2 sharpens the fear of uselessness. When Kazuma proposes a dungeon trip, she panics: "Don't wanna don't wanna, I'll lose my reason for existence if I go to a dungeon! If Explosion magic that might cause the dungeon to collapse can't be used, I'll become a normal person!" (v2 L3328). Kazuma counters by quoting her own recruitment promise: "Wasn't it something like, 'I'm willing to carry the luggage or anything when exploring dungeons! Please, don't throw me away!'" (v2 L3329). Cornered with her own recruitment promise, she resigns herself: "Sigh… I understand, but I'll really be useless, all right? I can only do things like carrying the bags…" — her expression carrying "a hint of unease" (v2 L3331-3332).
- Kazuma soothes this anxiety by reframing her role as still Explosion-relevant: "you just need to follow us until the entrance of the dungeon. If we meet any dangerous monsters on the way to the dungeon, I'll be relying on you to repel them with magic, all right?" (v2 L3334). She shifts from dread to baffled acceptance: "Hmm? Just the entrance? Will that really be fine?" (v2 L3335).
- At the dungeon entrance she repeats the same anxiety from the other direction, not wanting to burden Kazuma: "Me too, I would just be a burden if I follow… Think it over, Kazuma." (v2 L3385).
- The Winter Shogun sequence exposes a conflict between self-preservation and loyalty: she plays dead rather than face a monster she cannot affect (v2 L2773-2774), yet after Kazuma dies, she is the one sobbing over his body and refusing to leave (v2 L2903-2904, L2910). Her fear for herself and her attachment to the party coexist uneasily.
- The debt context in Section 2 also gives her a material motive: the party is "in debt" from the Beldia fight and sleeping in stables in winter (v2 L2654-2656), so the high-paying Snow Sprite hunt is practical survival, and Megumin's Explosion is what makes the haul lucrative (v2 L2760-2765).

## Knowledge, beliefs, secrets, and uncertainties

- She knows the skill system and explains it accurately to Kazuma (v1 L705-706).
- She knows undead/lich lore well: she explains why liches are dangerous — high magic defense, status effects on touch, life and mana drain, "a legendary immortal monster" (v1 L1248).
- She is honest about her made-up eyepatch lore (v1 L491), and her false "Crimson Demons die without daily Explosion" claim is exposed as bluffing (v1 L1526-1527).
- Beneath the confidence there is real uncertainty: she sweats when she realizes the castle belongs to a Demon King's General (v1 L1499); she is "intimidated and a bit scared" before the Dullahan (v1 L1508); and she is genuinely frightened of being abandoned (v1 L595).
- In Volume 2 she supplies practical monster lore. On Snow Sprites: "Snow Sprites are weak monsters. They can be found in places with lots of snow, and will shatter when hit with a sword" (v2 L2706), and "defeating one will allow spring to arrive half a day earlier" (v2 L2708). She has no objection to hunting them (v2 L2710).
- She can explain the Winter Shogun's status and bounty, and the difference between danger-based and threat-based pricing: "The Winter Shogun is a monster that doesn't do anything if you leave the Snow Sprites alone. Even so, the bounty should be about 200 million. As a Demon King's General, Beldia, had a high bounty because of how dangerous he was. For the Winter Shogun who doesn't actively attack humans to have a bounty of 200 million… it shows just how strong the Winter Shogun is." (v2 L2942).
- She understands fairy metaphysics well enough to assess her own spell's limits: "Fairies are immaterial, an existence just like mana. If it was an existence like the Fairy King, its magical defense would be rather high. Although Explosion could deal damage to all sort of existences, it would still be hard to settle it in one blow…" (v2 L2947).
- She is not omniscient about monsters, and says so plainly: "I don't know what Snow Sprites feed on. Really, what do they eat?" (v2 L2968).
- With Darkness, she is also a source of local knowledge about the approaching "Destroyer": the two explain it as "the huge and fast mobile fortress" that "rampages around and is strangely popular among the kids" (v2 L2698-2699; narration credits both: "Darkness and Megumin's explanation," v2 L2701).
- She persists in doubting Aqua's divine origin. In Section 3, Aqua herself notes, "although Megumin and Darkness still don't believe me" (v2 L3427), confirming that Megumin's earlier skepticism (v1 L1966-1968) has not softened by this point.
- Her early-morning disappearances remain a genuine uncertainty for Kazuma: "Megumin went somewhere early in the morning. She would disappear like that sometimes. I wonder where she goes?" (v2 L3675-3676). Within this pass, the text does not reveal what she is doing or where she goes.

## Personality hypotheses (behavior-backed)

1. **Theatrical / chuunibyou.** She repeatedly makes self-aggrandizing declarations, flicks her cloak, and uses dramatic phrasing ("Feast your eyes…"; "Behold my power!") (v1 L478, L882, L2097, L2582). Kazuma now literally labels her "a chuuni Archwizard" (v2 L2679), and she still cannot resist showing off: "Let me show you my power" before wasting her only Explosion on empty grass for a new audience (v2 L3312).
2. **Obsessive single-mindedness.** Her identity is fused with Explosion; she refuses utility magic and spends resources on making Explosion stronger (v1 L580, L1318-1321, L1343-1348). In Volume 2 she calls Explosion the thing that gives her "a reason for existence" and dreads becoming "a normal person" without it (v2 L3328), yet she can also refuse to cast it when the opponent frightens her (v2 L2947).
3. **Stubborn pride mixed with insecurity.** She bristles at slights to her name, race, or appearance (v1 L496-505, L1077, L1510-1521), yet clings desperately to the party (v1 L595). In the tavern she insists insults against her don't matter (v2 L2999), but she still gasps when Kazuma says there are no pretty ladies present (v2 L3020), and she is uneasy at being reduced to a bag-carrier (v2 L3331-3332).
4. **Playful and manipulative in a childish way.** She uses public misunderstanding to pressure Kazuma: "I'm fine with any sort of play! Even using the frog's slime just now is okay…" (v1 L605). She frames an innocent mage and dumps the Dullahan on Aqua (v1 L1493-1495, L1534). Her dungeon protest is similarly childish and evasive: "Don't wanna." followed by a physical attempt to run (v2 L3323, L3325).
5. **Genuinely loyal when it matters.** She is willing to walk into an undead-filled castle alone to lift Darkness's curse (v1 L1578-1579), and she relaxes with gratitude after being carried and praised (v1 L2115-2117). In Volume 2 she sobs over Kazuma's body (v2 L2903-2904), hugs him tightly when he revives (v2 L2914), and immediately checks on his body (v2 L2927).
6. **Competitive about titles and recognition.** She wants "Dragon Slayer Megumin" and the title of the strongest mage; she enjoys the crowd's awe but also jokingly wants to retaliate against hecklers (v1 L2101-2102, L2111-2115, L2563, L2626). In Section 3, recognition from a new leader is enough to make her spend her only spell: Dust praised her and she immediately said, "Let me show you my power" (v2 L3312).
7. **Cowardly self-preservation behind the bravado.** Faced with a monster her spent spell cannot hurt, she "was now playing dead" (v2 L2773-2774) and is praised by Kazuma for doing so "so perfectly" (v2 L2813); she also refuses to Explosion the Winter Shogun because "I don't want to cast Explosion on such a scary opponent" (v2 L2947).
8. **Affectionate and emotionally invested in the party.** Her reaction to Kazuma's death is raw, physical grief rather than a pose: sobbing on his corpse (v2 L2903-2904), wordless hugging on revival (v2 L2914), and a quiet worried question about whether his body feels wrong (v2 L2927). She tries to shield Kazuma from tavern insults even at her own expense (v2 L2999). In Section 4, when Kazuma and Aqua return from the dungeon bickering, her first response is a dry, knowing question rather than alarm — "… I can probably guess what happened, but allow me to ask anyway. What the hell happened?" (v2 L3658-3659) — and she then "stroked the head of Aqua who was sobbing behind me to console her" (v2 L3661). The caretaking is non-theatrical and extends beyond Kazuma to Aqua.
9. **Identity crisis when separated from Explosion.** She equates being unable to cast Explosion with existential uselessness: "I'll lose my reason for existence… I'll become a normal person!" (v2 L3328), and she preemptively frames herself as dead weight in the dungeon (v2 L3331-3332, L3385). The crisis is strong enough that she first tries to flee the conversation entirely (v2 L3325), and it eases only when Kazuma reassures her that her Explosion still has a use on the escort route (v2 L3334-3335).

## Dialogue and interaction styles

- Catchphrase-like self-introductions: "My name is Megumin!" followed by job/title and Explosion credentials (v1 L478, L882, L2097, L2594, L2626).
- Uses a smug "Kuku" laugh when proud of her magic (v1 L925) and "Humph" when posturing (v1 L489, L589).
- Banters sharply with Kazuma, including mock-offense and threats: "Crimson Demons would gladly take on anyone wanting a fight. Good, let's take this outside" (v1 L1077), and quick comebacks: "Crimson Demons have high intelligence… Want me to guess what Kazuma is thinking?" (v1 L2122-2124).
- Speaks of Explosion in poetic/sensory terms and invites shared appreciation (v1 L1468, L1471).
- Can be deadpan and knowing, e.g., telling Mitsurugi the sword is no longer with Kazuma, prompting the reveal "I sold it" (v1 L1945-1955), and joining in the flat skepticism of Aqua's goddess confession (v1 L1966-1968).
- In Volume 2, she asks permission in the field rather than acting unilaterally: "Can I use Explosion to blow up an area?" (v2 L2752).
- She reports battle results in a proud, quantified register even while collapsed: "Eight! I took out 8. And I raised a level!" (v2 L2761).
- When Kazuma revives, her register shifts to plain, soft concern: "… Is your body okay? Does it feel weird anywhere?" (v2 L2927).
- She can deliver confident expository lore (v2 L2706, L2942, L2947) and is equally comfortable admitting ignorance in casual conversation: "I don't know what Snow Sprites feed on. Really, what do they eat?" (v2 L2968).
- In the tavern, she uses a calming, de-escalating tone to manage Kazuma's anger: "Kazuma, don't bother with them. I won't mind no matter what they say about me." (v2 L2999).
- In the dungeon discussion, her protest is blunt and childlike — "Don't wanna." (v2 L3323) — and her surrender is a self-deprecating sigh: "Sigh… I understand, but I'll really be useless, all right? I can only do things like carrying the bags…" (v2 L3331). Once reassured, she switches to a puzzled, practical check: "Hmm? Just the entrance? Will that really be fine?" (v2 L3335).
- At the dungeon entrance, her concern for Kazuma is gentle and deferential: "Me too, I would just be a burden if I follow… Think it over, Kazuma." (v2 L3384-3385).
- In the Section 4 cabin scene, her opener is a composed, near-deadpan request for information delivered before any greeting: "… I can probably guess what happened, but allow me to ask anyway. What the hell happened?" (v2 L3658-3659), followed by wordless consolation as she strokes Aqua's head (v2 L3661).

## Directional relationships

- **Toward Kazuma:** physically clingy at recruitment (v1 L588-605); treats him as transport after casting (v1 L1435, L2103-2106, L2586); uses him as an audience and fellow Explosion appreciator (v1 L1468-1471); wants him to learn Explosion (v1 L714); teases and tests him (v1 L2122-2126); and trusts his battle commands (v1 L2088). In Volume 2 she asks his tactical permission before casting (v2 L2752), is devastated by his death (v2 L2903-2904), hugs him on revival (v2 L2914), worries over his body (v2 L2927), and tries to calm him down when outsiders insult him (v2 L2999). In Section 3 she tries to flee his dungeon proposal and is caught by the sleeve (v2 L3325), is soothed when he assures her she will only escort and use Explosion en route (v2 L3334-3335), and worries at the dungeon entrance about burdening him: "Think it over, Kazuma" (v2 L3385). In Section 4, Kazuma's party-balance aside frames her as his high-burst finisher rather than a source of reliable damage (v2 L3680-3681), and he notes that he does not know where she goes on her unexplained early-morning absences (v2 L3675-3676).
- **Toward Aqua:** initially regards the "goddess" claim with sympathy (v1 L516-519); bonds with her over impractical passion (v1 L581); uses Aqua as muscle ("Master, I leave it to you," v1 L1534); becomes her accomplice in continuing the castle explosions (v1 L2003-2006); and joins in skeptical dismissal of Aqua's goddess backstory (v1 L1966-1968). In Section 2, the two overlap in grief and caretaking over Kazuma's body (Megumin sobs on him while Aqua supports his head, v2 L2903-2913), and Aqua speaks of Megumin as "that child" who is "so stubborn" (v2 L2910); later they share idle curiosity about Snow Sprite diet (v2 L2967-2968). In Section 3, Aqua herself confirms the skepticism persists: "although Megumin and Darkness still don't believe me" (v2 L3427). In Section 4, Megumin is the one who soothes Aqua after the dungeon incident — "… I can probably guess what happened, but allow me to ask anyway. What the hell happened?" (v2 L3658-3659) — and consoles her by stroking her head while she sobs (v2 L3661), showing warmth toward Aqua even amid the others' bickering.
- **Toward Darkness:** with Aqua, is curious about Darkness at first (v1 L861-864); shares her hesitation about letting Wiz the lich go before agreeing Wiz is harmless (v1 L1235); goes pale with guilt when Darkness is cursed in her place (v1 L1557); is willing to risk herself to save Darkness (v1 L1578-1584); and later bickers with her about whether it is too early to drink (v1 L2530-2532). In Section 2, the two act together as the party's pursuers during the Snow Sprite hunt (v2 L2743-2753), jointly explain the Destroyer (v2 L2698-2701), and together hug Kazuma upon his revival (v2 L2914). In Section 3 they are again paired as the non-combatants left behind at the refuge while Kazuma and Aqua enter the dungeon (v2 L3420), and both are named by Aqua as disbelievers in her goddess identity (v2 L3427).
- **Toward other adventurers and townsfolk:** enjoys public attention for her magic but is stung by backhanded praise like "girl with a screw loose" and jokingly wants to cast Explosion on the hecklers (v1 L2111-2115). In Volume 2, she is calm and conciliatory toward the drunk Dust's provocation, telling Kazuma not to bother with him (v2 L2998-2999), but when Kazuma denies that his party members are "pretty ladies," Megumin joins the other two in gasping and pointing at herself (v2 L3020).
- **Toward a temporary leader (Dust):** in the swap aftermath, she seeks praise and immediately proves her worth by spending her only Explosion on empty grassland — "Let me show you my power" — which leaves her drained when the Rookie Killer appears and ends with her being "carried on Dust's back" (v2 L3309, L3312-3314).
