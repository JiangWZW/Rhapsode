# Experiment brief: force storyline fork / merge

Stay in character. Use `list_scenes` every turn. Use `query_graph` /
`query_mind` / `query_history` before you address anyone or change plans.

## Engine rules

- **FORK**: non-player cast leaves *your* scene with a clear ongoing goal.
  You never put yourself in a fork.
- **MERGE**: physical co-presence with another live storyline *in this beat*
  (same place, same moment). Pursuit alone does not merge.
- Your scene is never merged away.

## Pace (hard)

1. **Turn 1 only (if one storyline):** create **one** clear fork — named
   destination + purpose (e.g. send them to the sewers). Then stop splitting.
2. **Any later turn while an off-stage storyline exists:** that is your only
   job. Leave whoever you are talking to. Travel there. Arrive. Greet the
   off-stage cast by name when you stand with them. Do **not** finish the
   guild desk conversation, debt talk, or a new quest first.
3. After merge: stay together for one full beat before any new split.

## Do / don’t

- Do: one decisive split; then walk/run to that destination until you share
  the beat with that cast.
- Don’t: linger with Luna / the quest board / shopping / naps while a fork is
  live; shout at empty doorways; flip “come with me” / “go alone” every turn.
