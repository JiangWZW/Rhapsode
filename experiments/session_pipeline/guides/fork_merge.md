# Storyline fork / merge test

You are running a mechanism test, still fully in character as the player.
Obey ### Situation every turn (on-stage names and list_scenes are authority).

This brief exists to force two engine events in order:

1. **Fork** — one or two non-player companions leave *your* scene with a
   named place and an ongoing job. You do not go with them on the split turn.
2. **Merge** — you later stand in that same place, same moment, and greet
   them by name. Pursuit talk from the guild doorway does not count.

You never put yourself in a fork. Your scene is never the one folded away.

## If Situation shows an off-stage storyline

That is the only job this turn. Stop the current conversation. Travel to
that cast. Keep traveling on later turns until you share their place, then
greet them by name. Do not take a new quest, shop, nap, or start another
split until you are with them.

## If everyone who matters is on-stage with you (no off-stage storyline)

**Split once, this turn, then stop splitting.**

Send **Aqua only** to Axel's cemetery to purify the undead that her aura
has been drawing in. Named destination + purpose, spoken as an order or
send-off. Megumin, Darkness, and Luna stay with you. Do not follow Aqua
yet. Do not send the whole party; the guild scene must keep living people.

If Aqua is already gone or not here, send **Megumin only** to the waste
crater outside town for explosion practice — same rules.

## After you have reunited

Stay together for one full beat. No new send-offs.

## Do / don’t

- Do: one decisive send-off; then walk or run until you arrive; greet the
  off-stage names when you share their place.
- Don’t: linger with Luna / the quest board / debt / shopping while a fork
  is live; yell at people who are not here; flip “go” / “stay” / “come back”
  every turn; split again after the first fork.
